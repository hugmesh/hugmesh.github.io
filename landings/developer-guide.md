For people who change HugMesh. Start with
[Add a service to the mesh](/developer-guide/expanding-the-mesh), which explains how a new
service reaches a running cluster.

Each service has its own repository under
[github.com/hugmesh](https://github.com/hugmesh).

To see what is being worked on and what is planned next, open the
[project board](/board): a list of the open tasks and bugs in the public repositories.
