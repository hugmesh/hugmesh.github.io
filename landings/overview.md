HugMesh runs a community's services on hardware its members own: Matrix chat, a
fediverse server, a forum, a wiki, video, live streaming and games, behind one sign-in
that you run yourself.

This tab is two pages. Read [What HugMesh is](/overview/what-is-hugmesh) first, then
[How it works](/overview/how-it-works). When you are ready to build, go to
[Get started](/tutorials).
